# Dict 1.0 (build 12)

This build fixes three things that made dictation feel unreliable.

## Your last words make it in

If you let go of the dictation key while you were still finishing a sentence, the end of it could go missing, sometimes several words. Dict now keeps listening after you let go until you've actually stopped talking, then inserts everything you said. If you'd already finished, nothing changes. If you were mid-sentence, the text shows up a moment later with nothing cut off.

## Fewer stray question marks

In hands-free mode, Dict tidies up punctuation for you, and it had a habit of ending ordinary statements with a question mark ("I think we're in good shape?"). Now a question mark only appears when what you said reads like a question.

## No extra space on empty lines in Obsidian

With "Add a space before dictation when needed" turned on, dictating onto a fresh line in Obsidian could start with a stray space. Dict now recognizes the start of a line there and leaves the space out.
